About Me

I work in enterprise IT support, where my day-to-day spans the full lifecycle of user technology. I provision and prepare endpoints, troubleshoot hardware issues, and handle Active Directory account support so people can get back to work quickly.

Much of my work runs through ServiceNow, where I manage incidents and keep asset records accurate as equipment moves through an organization. I also provide hands-on, physical assistance to infrastructure teams, which has given me a close look at how the systems behind the help desk are built and maintained.

That exposure is what drives my next step. I'm interested in infrastructure operations, networking, and data center technology, and I'm continuing to grow my skills in those areas through coursework and hands-on projects.

Educational prototype

Smart Email Router

A prototype that classifies incoming university inquiries and routes each one to the right team: IT, Billing, or Admissions.

How it works

  • Reads the content of an inquiry and assigns it to one of three categories.
  • Sends requests that don't clearly fit a category to manual review instead of guessing.
  • Keeps a person involved in ambiguous cases so unclear requests are not misrouted.
  • Text classification
  • Request routing
  • Human-in-the-loop review
Coursework prototype · RAG

Open-Book IT Policy Assistant

A retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) prototype that answers questions about fictional university IT policies using the policy text itself.

How it works

  • Retrieves the policy sections most relevant to a user's question.
  • Generates an answer grounded in the retrieved sections rather than general knowledge.
  • Works "open book," so answers stay tied to the source policy content.
  • Retrieval-augmented generation
  • Document retrieval
  • Grounded answers
